# Third Peak Ltd.

## Get Unstuck. Get to Market. Build What Matters.

-----

### The Story

I’m a biochemist turned technologist turned business strategist who spent twenty years getting really good at things that weren’t quite the thing.

My first real job was building AI software to help discover cures for fatal diseases. It was the intersection of everything I loved — artificial intelligence, life sciences, and the fundamental question of what makes human intelligence unique. It was the best work I’ve ever done. And when the company failed, not because the science was wrong but because the business couldn’t hold it up, it taught me a lesson I’ve carried ever since: mission without business viability dies on the vine.

So I went and learned the other side. I spent two decades in technology leadership and business strategy — building products, scaling organizations, taking things to market across industries and company sizes. I got good at it. I got paid well for it. But the whole time, I was circling back to the thing that originally lit me up: using AI and technology to help people live healthier, fuller lives.

Now I’m at a point where I’ve got the skills, the perspective, and the urgency to get back to that intersection. Not just treating disease after it shows up, but understanding how intelligence, behavior, and biology intersect to keep people well in the first place.

That’s what Third Peak is.

-----

### Why “Third Peak”

Every career has its chapters. My first peak was the discovery — falling in love with AI and science and the big questions about life and intelligence. My second was the long climb through business and technology leadership, learning how to build things that scale and last. This is the third — the one I’m choosing deliberately, where everything I’ve learned converges on work that actually matters.

The name is also personal. I love mountains. I love skiing. A peak is something you earn through effort, and the view from the top changes how you see everything below.

### What I Do

**Third Peak Consulting** helps mission-driven health and wellness ventures get unstuck, get to market, and scale.

You have a powerful mission. Real science. A genuine desire to improve people’s lives. But something is stalling — strategy misalignment, unclear go-to-market, technology decisions that aren’t keeping pace with your ambition, organizational dynamics that slow everything down.

I’ve seen this pattern dozens of times across startups, nonprofits, and mission-driven organizations. Brilliant people with important ideas who can’t bridge the gap between what they’re building and the people who need it. I help close that gap. Not as an outside advisor handing you a slide deck — as a strategic partner who understands both the mission and the mechanics of getting there.

What that looks like:

- **Strategic clarity** — aligning your mission, market, and business model so you stop waffling and start moving
- **Go-to-market strategy** — turning your vision into a concrete path to reaching and serving real people
- **Technology and AI strategy** — making smart, scalable technology decisions that serve the mission rather than distract from it
- **Organizational alignment** — helping leadership teams get out of their own way and execute with focus

**Third Peak Studio** builds the technology that brings it all to life.

Strategy without execution is just conversation. The studio side of Third Peak is where ideas become real — software applications, agentic AI tools, data integrations, and platforms that serve the health and wellness space. Whether it’s a product built for a consulting client or our own tools designed to solve problems we see across the industry, the studio exists to build things that matter.

### What I Believe

Every business is a technology business now. That’s table stakes. The harder question is whether your technology is in service of something that matters.

Healthcare is cheaper and better than disease care. The future belongs to those who help people thrive proactively — through lifestyle, behavior, environment, and intelligent intervention — not just treat what’s already broken.

The best ideas in the world die every day because of waffling, misalignment, and the gap between mission and execution. That gap is solvable. It just takes someone who can hold both sides — the vision and the mechanics — at the same time.

AI is not a feature. It’s a fundamental shift in what’s possible for human health and wellbeing. The people who understand both the technology and the humanity it serves will define what comes next.
